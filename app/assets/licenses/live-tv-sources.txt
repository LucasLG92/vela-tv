LIVE TELEVISION IN VELA

Channel information and programme guide: TDTChannels, by Marc Vila / LaQuay and contributors.
https://github.com/LaQuay/TDTChannels
https://www.tdtchannels.com/
Catalog: https://www.tdtchannels.com/lists/tv.json
Guide: https://www.tdtchannels.com/epg/TV.json
Repository license: Apache 2.0, included in tdtchannels-Apache-2.0.txt.
The catalog and guide are fetched at runtime; no television recordings are bundled or redistributed.
Channel names, logos and broadcasts belong to their respective owners.

Vela's native browser and player are independent implementations. Vela is not affiliated with TDTChannels or TDT Spain.
TDT Spain's public M3U manual was consulted for format interoperability:
https://www.tdtspain.com/m3u.html
Personal playlists support standard EXTINF metadata and direct HTTPS signals. Custom extraction rules,
DRM, cookies and provider authentication are not imported from TDT Spain or other applications.
Some broadcasters require their own application, website, account or geographic availability.

From Vela 1.9.1 official channel websites can open inside Vela using Android System WebView,
loaded on demand in a separate process. Broadcaster players, consent, advertising and account/DRM
checks remain under their control. Web sessions stay privately on the device and are independent
of sessions in other applications. Availability in a WebView depends on the broadcaster and device.
Android WebView documentation: https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/layout/webapps/webview
