LIVE TELEVISION IN VELA

Channel information and programme guide: TDTChannels, by Marc Vila / LaQuay and contributors.
https://github.com/LaQuay/TDTChannels
https://www.tdtchannels.com/
Catalog: https://www.tdtchannels.com/lists/tv.json
Guide: https://www.tdtchannels.com/epg/TV.json
Repository license: Apache 2.0, included in tdtchannels-Apache-2.0.txt.
The catalog and guide are fetched at runtime; no television recordings are bundled or redistributed.
Channel names, logos and broadcasts belong to their respective owners.

Vela's native channel browser, M3U parser and player are independent implementations.
Initial M3U playlist: https://www.tdtchannels.com/lists/tv.m3u8
Personal M3U and Xtream lists use their chosen provider. Only direct media signals are played.
Antenna channels use the Android TV Input Framework and the manufacturer's existing tuner catalog.
https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/tv/TvView
No website player or other IPTV application is used by this integration.

External XMLTV guide: EPG dobleM, by davidmuma.
https://github.com/davidmuma/EPG_dobleM
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/davidmuma/EPG_dobleM/master/guiatv.xml
The guide is fetched at runtime and indexed privately on the device. No XMLTV dataset is bundled.
Programme metadata and channel trademarks remain with their respective owners.

Missing provider logos can use the original channel icon declared by the selected XMLTV.
EPG dobleM channel icons: https://github.com/davidmuma/picons_dobleM
