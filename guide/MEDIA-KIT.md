# Vela media kit

**A personal home for Android TV and Google TV.** Vela brings Plex/Jellyfin libraries, live television, apps and HDMI devices into one remote-first interface. One APK offers Full and Lite modes.

## Short description

Vela is a free, open-source Android TV launcher for your own entertainment. Bring Plex or Jellyfin, live channels, apps and HDMI together; choose a simpler Lite mode when your television mainly serves a box or console.

## What to show first

1. **Your collection on Home.** Original artwork, Continue watching with remaining time, recently added films/series, and an editor for row order, names, sizes and sources.
2. **Live TV inside the launcher.** A horizontal guide, favourites, programme discovery and channel browsing without leaving playback. Full accepts M3U, Xtream and antenna; Lite uses the television's antenna.
3. **One app, two useful modes.** Full for a personal entertainment home; Lite for HDMI, antenna and apps. Both keep the sidebar, integrated settings and custom remote actions.
