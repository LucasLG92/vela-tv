## Night cinema comes to Vela

In Full, **Night cinema** brings portrait posters, landscape Continue watching cards and playback progress to the Library Home. Hold OK on Home and choose a presentation in the editor. Existing presentation choices stay with the profile.

The new graphite sidebar opens over the scene in both Full and Lite. Icons stay on the same vertical line. Use Up and Down to explore, OK to enter a section, and Right or Back to return to the content. On the pinned YouTube shortcut, Right still opens its video shelf and OK launches the app.

Pinned apps retain their original artwork, order and long-press actions. The sidebar respects reduced motion and the chosen focus colour. Night cinema’s clock and weather sit directly over the artwork.

Install the unified APK over Vela, or open **Settings → Updates** in the launcher. Android 8+ supports Lite. Full requires Android 11+.
