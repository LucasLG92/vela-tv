## More of the picture in the TV guide

The selected programme photo keeps its original proportions and its full frame, including the top of the shot. Colours from the photo extend behind the programme details. A gradual fade continues behind the time ruler and ends at the first channel row. Both Full and Lite use the same framing.

If the artwork server times out, Vela retries once while the same listing is selected. Changing programmes cancels the pending retry. A listing without a photo keeps the plain guide background.

Open **Settings → Updates** to install the unified preview.
