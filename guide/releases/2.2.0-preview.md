# Vela 2.2 Preview · Make Home yours

Build a Home around the things you actually watch. Choose your rows, their order and their services, with an editor that stays inside Vela.

- **Your own Home layout:** show, hide, rename, reorder and resize the available sections. Hold OK to move a section, use the arrows and press OK to place it. Back undoes the move; Save and Cancel control the complete draft. Every profile keeps its own choices.
- **Your own Continue watching:** choose Plex, Jellyfin and individual installed apps independently of the rest of Home. Return to automatic service selection at any time. Plex and Jellyfin require a linked library; other apps contribute only the Watch Next metadata they share with Android TV. Enabling an app does not expose a private history it does not publish.
- **A source for each row:** follow the Home library, the current catalogue library, Plex, Jellyfin or both. Add separate recently added movies, recently added series, unwatched and top-rated rows. Keep collections and widgets in their existing editors.
- **Three ways to settle in:** Cinema, Compact or Rows only. Rows only releases the large header image and gives the shelves more room.
- **Focus that belongs in Vela:** a fine accent outline, short heading underline and light neighbour tint keep the selected poster clear. Reduced motion retains static contrast cues; high contrast remains available. Quickly returning to a previous title now cancels a late backdrop for another title.
- **Lite gets the same editor:** rename, resize, hide and reorder HDMI, antenna and Apps destinations, with its media-free scope preserved.

The existing artwork cache and cancellable workers are reused. Additional feeds are opt-in and bounded to twelve titles; the editor uses a recycled list. No background service, blur layer, extra artwork cache or permanent focus animation is added. Settings backups and sync include the new layout choices while excluding account credentials.

Validation uses native Android 16 and Android 8 emulators, synthetic profiles and authorised demonstration artwork. Tests cover remote navigation, draft cancellation, Save, service defaults, independent providers, stale-account actions, a delayed backdrop, reduced motion and an empty Home without feed requests. Physical-device performance and external-app history availability remain device/service dependent.

One signed APK includes Full on Android 11+ and Lite on Android 8+. Existing profiles and linked accounts remain saved. The standalone Lite 0.18.3 compatibility update includes its editor and retains the explicit migration route to unified Vela.
