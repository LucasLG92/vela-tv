# Vela 1.35.1 Preview

Your library, channels, apps and devices, with less visual clutter.

- Recent activity now lives in Apps, beside drawer controls, and is reachable with the remote. Home artwork stays clear.
- The in-app logo appears once, in Settings. The refined native vector uses graphite and one restrained accent.
- Cinematic artwork and integrated menu backgrounds stay full-screen when navigation opens.
- Refined settings, remote focus, Cinema/Compact/Minimal styles, complete synopses, library details, paged seasons/episodes and Plex/Jellyfin collections.
- Guide paging selects the correct next channel; EPG corrections update the logo; What to watch is reachable with the remote.
- Optional content frame-rate matching and reusable player controls keep the existing cache and decoder budgets.

Download **Vela-1.35.1-preview.apk** below. Full Vela requires **Android 11+**. Existing installations can check Settings → Updates. Android asks you to confirm installation.

The APK retains the official Vela signing identity. Its SHA-256 checksum is attached. Source, APK contents and public screenshots have been reviewed for personal information; screenshots use demonstration profiles, a demonstration location and authorised original artwork. No account, IPTV credentials or signing key is bundled.
