## A lighter app drawer

Apps open directly into the grid in Full and Lite. In Full, a star beside the app name marks each sidebar favorite. The focus outline shows which app will open when you press OK.

Choose **Organize** to manage folders and layout. In Lite, press Up from the first row to reach Organize or Recent activity.
