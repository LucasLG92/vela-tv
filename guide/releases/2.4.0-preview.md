# Vela 2.4.0 Preview · Start with what you watch

Choose your Vela mode and preview a Home layout with the remote on the first launch. Accounts and TV sources can be set up later.

- **Library** puts Continue watching and recently added Plex and Jellyfin titles up front. Open the app drawer while your library is unlinked.
- **Television** highlights your favourites and what is on now in Full, with one step to the previous channel or the programme guide. Lite starts in its native antenna section.
- **HDMI and apps** puts your connected devices and installed app shortcuts on the same screen.
- **Change your mind anytime.** Hold OK on **Home** in Full or **HDMI** in Lite, then open **Home layouts** in the editor to try another layout or restore the previous one. Your layout and its history belong to the current profile.

The layout preview uses simple native drawing and starts no media clients. The shortcuts row loads up to five app icons. Menus are available in all six Vela languages.

Update from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**, or install the official signed APK over Vela. [Installation guide](https://github.com/LucasLG92/vela-tv/blob/main/guide/INSTALL.md).
