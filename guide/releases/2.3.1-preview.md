# Vela 2.3.1 Preview · A steadier television guide

This hotfix keeps the guide aligned when you leave the current or next programme selected.

- **Stable focus.** Programme cards keep the same size, title baseline and schedule position when focus enters or leaves. Selection changes the highlight without pushing the information around.
- **Room for long titles.** A selected title uses a second line only when it fits above the schedule, including larger reading preferences. The progress bar stays inside the card.
- **Quieter refreshes.** While the same channels and time window refresh, the existing bounded guide remains visible. Completed empty responses, different channel records and new time windows still clear unavailable data.

The correction is shared by Full and Lite. It adds no background service, animation timer, artwork cache or playback engine.

Native regression tests reproduce the previous title jump and verify current/next selection, repeated idle redraws, 100/115/130% reading sizes, refresh retention and stale-data clearing on Android 8 and Android 16.

Update from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**, or install the attached official APK over Vela. Android asks you to confirm installation; your accounts and preferences stay in place.
