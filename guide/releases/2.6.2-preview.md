## A clearer programme guide

The selected programme’s artwork fits inside a widescreen preview at the top right. Faces, captions and the edges of the picture stay in frame. Artwork appears when supplied by the listings.

Live TV opens on the category list. Use **Up** and **Down** to browse categories while the channels load, then **Right** to enter the schedule. The guide keeps the current and upcoming programme titles separate when an XMLTV feed supplies several titles for one broadcast. Existing guide caches refresh automatically.

## Three ways to browse HDMI

Choose **Device cards**, **Preview window** or **Cinema backdrop** when setting up an HDMI Home. Change the selection later from **HDMI inputs** or **Settings → Appearance**. Each profile remembers its choice. Live previews use the signal exposed by the television and stay muted until you open the input. TVs without preview support show the device icon.

Update from **Settings → Updates** or install the signed unified APK over your current installation. It includes both Full and Lite.
