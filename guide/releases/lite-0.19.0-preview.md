# Vela Lite 0.19.0 Preview · Two ways to start

Choose **Television** for your antenna channels, or **HDMI and apps** for connected devices and installed app shortcuts. Open **Edit home sections → Home layouts** to preview a choice with the remote, save it or restore your previous Home.

This was the last standalone Lite build. Its APK remains available for profile migration. All future updates are delivered through [unified Vela](https://github.com/LucasLG92/vela-tv/releases). [Migration instructions](https://github.com/LucasLG92/vela-tv/blob/main/guide/UNIFIED.md#moving-from-the-old-standalone-lite-app).
