## TV guide artwork

Select a programme in the guide to see its photo across the header. Full and Lite keep the photo supplied by the online guide when antenna listings also arrive. They can also use a matching tuner listing's artwork. M3U guides now retain programme pictures and Xtream listings can supply their own. Listings without a picture keep the plain header.

## HDMI layouts

Open **Settings → Appearance → HDMI layout** to choose **Device cards**, **Preview window** or **Cinema backdrop** with the remote. The choice is saved for your profile. The Inputs screen now opens directly on the ports.

Open **Settings → Updates** to install this update in either mode.
