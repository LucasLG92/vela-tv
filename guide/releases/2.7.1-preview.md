## Programme photos for existing TV guides

If your TV showed the title and time but the guide header stayed blank, Vela now recognises the public dobleM `guiatv.xml` and `guiatv.xml.gz` feeds and requests the matching edition with programme photos. Open Live TV after updating. The first background import can take a little while.

Your channel list, favourites and guide corrections keep the same source identity. If the artwork feed is unavailable, existing listings remain readable. On a fresh installation Vela can fall back to the text feed and retry the artwork later. A programme gets a photo when its listing supplies one.

Install the unified APK from **Settings → Updates**.
