# Vela 2.1.1 Preview · Clearer voice, closer to your library

- **Exterior voice halo:** a soft, speech-responsive contour follows the outside of the sidebar, collapsed or expanded. The microphone stays small and neutral. Interior ribbons and microphone blooms are removed. Reduced motion keeps a static contour.
- **Specific Plex titles:** catalogue cards use item-specific Android library-detail links, with a separate series route and compatibility with legacy Android clients. Season metadata retains its parent series. Existing Continue watching handoffs stay preferred when Android can resolve them.
- **Clear launch failures:** Vela no longer disguises a failed catalogue handoff by opening Plex Home instead. Links carry only server and item identifiers, without tokens or server addresses.

Native Vela tests cover clicks across Home shelves, movie/series routing, unchanged resume handoffs, invalid identifiers and an exterior-only halo in both modes and sidebar sizes. The destination screen inside real Plex clients still needs verification on physical televisions; this preview is intended for that testing. No new background service, artwork cache or microphone recording is added.

One signed APK includes Full on Android 11+ and Lite on Android 8+. Existing settings and linked accounts stay in place. Standalone Lite 0.18.2 receives the shared exterior halo and keeps its explicit migration route.
