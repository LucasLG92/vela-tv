# Vela Lite 0.17.0 Preview · A clearer view of television

- Choose and arrange up to four Home widgets: clock, optional weather, date, your next followed programme or a useful shortcut. Widget choices belong to each local profile.
- Browse What to watch within the TV canvas. Now, Tonight and Next 48 hours have direct genre filters and six programme cards per page, with original EPG images or channel logos, remaining time and live progress.
- Keep TV actions below the widgets and clear of the guide's time scale. The main sidebar continues to identify this as TV, with a direct route back to the guide.
- Select Switch when it starts on a future guide programme. Schedule or cancel within the same programme card, with a clear confirmation and stable remote focus.
- Automatic switching works while Vela is visible. Missed starts while it is closed, in another app or in standby are skipped. Up to sixteen one-shot plans stay private to each profile; plans are excluded from backups and sync.
- Keep discovery bounded to twenty-four records per batch and six visible cards. Decode thumbnails separately from detail images within the existing 512 KiB artwork cache; stop disposable artwork workers and the foreground timer when paused. No new background service or permission.
- Keep the integrated graphite interface and all six menu languages. Existing accounts, apps and preferences are preserved on update.

Lite keeps antenna playback on the television tuner and contains no Plex, Jellyfin, M3U or Xtream runtime.

Install over the existing edition. The package and official signing certificate are unchanged. Android still asks you to confirm installation.
