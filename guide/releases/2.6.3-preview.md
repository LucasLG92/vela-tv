## Programme artwork across the guide

In Full and Lite the selected programme’s picture now spans the top of the TV guide. A dark gradient behind the channel, title and times keeps them readable. Landscape stills fill the header with their original proportions. Portrait posters keep their full height against colours from the same image. The guide clears the picture when the selected listing has no artwork.

## Arrange your Home

On the default Full Home, Continue watching is the first row and Recently added sits directly beneath it. Previously saved custom orders still apply. Hold **OK** on **Home**, open **Sections → Add section** and choose recent movies, recent series, unwatched movies or top-rated movies. New rows appear at the end. Move them with the remote and choose **Save layout** to apply the changes to your profile.

Open **Settings → Updates** to update either mode of the unified APK.
