# Vela 1.36.0 Preview

More of your library, from the moment you arrive.

- A more compact cinematic Home brings Recently added into view below Continue watching.
- Original platform logos sit inside each thumbnail. Remaining time appears just above playback progress, with episode information kept in the image.
- Two-line titles, original image proportions and remote navigation remain consistent across all three card sizes.
- Recent activity can return to an available channel without depending on the currently visible guide page.
- Voice and Android search requests reach the integrated search page. Explicit open commands, ambiguous results and app/channel/input searches use the same themed controls.
- Assign an available button to Search by voice in Remote & diagnostics when your television reserves the Google Assistant key.

Download **Vela-1.36.0-preview.apk** below. Requires **Android 11+**. Existing installations can use Settings → Updates; Android asks you to confirm installation. Your accounts and preferences remain on the television.

Verified with native emulator tests for mixed-provider cards, long titles, three sizes, fading edges, remote navigation, recent channels and recognition-result handling. Voice recognition requires a compatible installed recognizer; Android may keep control of the physical Assistant button. No new microphone permission, background service or image cache was added.

The APK keeps the official signing identity and includes a SHA-256 checksum attachment. Public images use demonstration profiles, location and playback progress with authorised original artwork. No personal account, IPTV credentials, signing key or private configuration is bundled.
