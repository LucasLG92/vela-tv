# Vela Lite 0.18.6 Preview · A steadier television guide

Hotfix for the separate legacy Lite package. Programme cards no longer resize or move their title and schedule when selected. Long titles respect the space above the schedule, and refreshing the same channel window keeps the available information visible until the result arrives.

The shared native regression passes on Android 8, including larger reading sizes and stale-data clearing. No new service, timer, artwork cache or player is added.

Update over the existing separate Lite app. New installations should use the unified Vela APK and choose Lite.
