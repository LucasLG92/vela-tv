# Vela 2.3.0 Preview · Playback that learns

A more adaptable M3U and Xtream player, in the same quiet Vela interface.

- **Keep the working signal.** Automatic playback remembers a channel's successful format and engine after playback advances. A working TS stream can start first next time instead of repeatedly trying unavailable HLS.
- **Choose your balance.** Fast, Balanced and Stable profiles tune Media3 startup, rebuffering and encoded-buffer targets. Balanced is the default; light mode and Android low-RAM devices cap these targets at 4 MiB. This is a buffer target, not total app RAM.
- **Recover with limits.** Temporary network failures get a bounded retry. Decoder/format problems can switch to the system player after releasing Media3. Missing signals use the channel's existing alternatives. Access refusals stop after that variant's formats, with a clear message.
- **Respect your playlist.** Allow-listed User-Agent, Referer and Origin options travel with their own M3U signal, including HLS child requests and compatible local pause. They stay in the encrypted catalogue and out of portable backups.
- **One title, clearly shown.** Home, Movies and Series keep the supplied title logo or the readable text fallback, avoiding overlapping names after a layout change.

## Update

Open **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**, or install the attached official APK over Vela. Android asks you to confirm installation. Existing sources, libraries and preferences stay in place.

One APK offers **Full on Android 11+ and Lite on Android 8+**. IPTV playback changes apply to Full; Lite keeps HDMI, antenna and apps without starting the IPTV player. The separate Lite 0.18.5 transition build preserves its own update path.
