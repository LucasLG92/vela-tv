# Vela 1.38.2 Preview · Just the time

The clock widget now shows only the time, centred in its existing card. In 12-hour mode, AM/PM stays on the same line. Weather and your other chosen widgets keep their space. No new worker, permission or background service.

Includes integrated What to watch, Home widgets and scheduled channel switching. Full also includes the 1.38.1 indexed programme query fix for large M3U and Xtream catalogues.

Update over your existing installation to keep your accounts and preferences. Android asks you to confirm the installation.
