## A closer look at live television

Highlight a programme in the guide to see its artwork, broadcast time, progress and what follows. Both editions use the same graphite palette and fixed channel rows. Artwork comes from programme listings when available. Your own XMLTV guide remains selectable.

Open a channel to see its logo, the current show and what comes next. The overlay clears automatically. Press **OK** to bring it back, **Left** for playback controls or **Right** to browse channels while the picture keeps playing. Available audio, subtitles and picture options depend on the stream or TV tuner.

Movies and Series now carry the Home presentation through their headers and shelves. The app drawer, inputs, search, programme details and settings share its focus treatment. Returning from the sidebar also keeps the Play action visible on Home, Movies and Series.

Update from **Settings → Updates** or install the signed unified APK over your current Vela installation. The same download includes Full and Lite.
