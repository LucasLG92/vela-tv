# Vela Lite 0.15.0 Preview

Your television, closer to the next action.

- Recent activity can return directly to an available antenna channel and restore the television section.
- Voice and Android search requests use the integrated search controls, with channel, installed-app and HDMI routing.
- Search and explicit open commands are handled separately. Ambiguous requests keep the choice visible in the themed interface.
- Assign an available remote button to Search by voice in Remote & diagnostics when your television reserves its Assistant key.
- Recognition requests preserve the active profile across activity recreation, discard cancelled results and wait until the app is in the foreground before acting.

Download **Vela-Lite-0.15.0-preview.apk** below. Requires **Android 8.0+**. Existing installations can use Settings → Updates; Android asks you to confirm installation. Full and Lite remain separate Android packages.

Native emulator tests cover recent channels, themed search, remote keys and the external recognizer activity lifecycle. A compatible installed recognizer is required; Android may keep control of the physical Assistant button. Lite retains its independent tuner runtime, bounded caches and no background service, Plex/Jellyfin client, IPTV client or Media3 engine.

The APK keeps the official signing identity and includes a SHA-256 checksum attachment. No personal account, IPTV credentials, location or signing key is bundled.
