# Images and credits

The English gallery contains native Vela and Vela Lite views captured at 3840×2160 on an owned emulator. New Home and Continue watching captures show Vela 1.36.0-preview; each image records its version in the gallery manifest. Movie posters and backdrops were retrieved from Plex with the user's permission to publish interface screenshots. Profiles, library names, progress, pagination, connected-device states and Lite tuner programmes are demonstrations. The weather city is Reykjavík, explicitly labelled Demo.

Movie artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their appearance illustrates Vela and does not imply sponsorship or affiliation. The code's MIT licence **does not apply to that artwork**. Original Plex assets, private paths, credentials and library video files are not redistributed in this repository or the production APKs. Some original posters retain Spanish lettering; the main interface gallery and demonstration metadata are English. Additional language previews show French, German and Italian.

Titles include Oppenheimer, Inception, Avatar: Fire and Ash, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, Gladiator II, The Wild Robot, Superman, Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu, The Last of Us, Stranger Things, Breaking Bad and Death of a Unicorn. The mixed-platform Home examples also show an original Dune: Part Two trailer thumbnail and Reacher artwork. Playback progress and episode details are demonstration values, not private service history. Each installation uses its own library's artwork and availability.

Public TV screenshots use the [TDTChannels](https://github.com/LaQuay/TDTChannels) playlist/logos and [EPG dobleM](https://github.com/davidmuma/EPG_dobleM) schedules. Original names, logos and broadcast frames belong to their respective owners. Live playback captures illustrate an official public RTVE stream; no recording is included. Programme data changes over time. Lite guide/player screenshots use synthetic tuner metadata and do not claim a physical antenna signal.

App and device symbols follow the [brand sources](../app/assets/brands/sources.txt) and [device-icon notices](../app/assets/licenses/device-icons.txt). Some gallery apps are test stubs displaying their original icon; screenshots do not demonstrate private service sessions. The Plex linking code DEMO is deliberately non-functional.

Screensaver videos are linked rather than bundled; [sources](../app/assets/ambient/sources.txt). Weather uses Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0. No AI-generated artwork is used in the gallery.
